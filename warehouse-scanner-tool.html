Warehouse inventory scanner

Scan a Walmart shipping label with your phone camera to mark an order Delivered against your Google Sheet order database, plus simple Receive/Ship logging to track stock on hand per warehouse (IL, TX, NC, NJ, NY, SGL).

Not connected — click "Connect Google Sheet" to sign in.

Once the camera's on, it scans automatically in the background — just point it at the label. Tap Scan now only to force an immediate retry.
